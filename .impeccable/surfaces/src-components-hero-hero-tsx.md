# Hero — portfolio

Mode: Experience. Scope: the header/hero of the home page, shared world with #sobre.
Audience: recruiters and clients deciding whether Warney Rego is worth a conversation. Copy comes from src/data/portfolio.ts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A two-shade halftone poster, not a portfolio header: the name rides a printed sky the way a gig poster sells a night. Refuses the photo-plus-buttons hero.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned by the user's "MONOCHROME" poster reference. Exactly two shades, ink and paper, swapped by theme (dark: cream dots on black, the poster; light: black dots on cream). Everything pictorial is AM halftone, screened offline into alpha masks and painted with the tokens. Archivo at full width and black weight for display; small italic wide caps for labels; Bricolage for running text. Film grain on the ground.

STORY: See the sky split by light and a small winged figure rising into it, read the name and SOFTWARE ENGINEER, then the one-line pitch and the contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed halftone sky (clouds at edges and bottom, sun and rays upper right). Top: location, nav, theme toggle in small wide caps. Upper left: italic label with the name, then SOFTWARE / ENGINEER stacked, filling the width. Right under it, italic caps headline. Icarus small in the rays. Bottom: pitch left, e-mail and links right, ↗ in the corner.

FORM: Code-led; the user's poster is the reference comp. Plates: public/images/hero/sky.webp, rays.webp, icarus.webp, icarus-shape.webp.

SIGNATURE: Icarus climbs toward the sun as the page scrolls (scroll-driven, idle float elsewhere); the rays breathe. Seed: user reference (monochrome halftone poster).
